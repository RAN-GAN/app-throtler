App Throttler slows, blocks and inspects the traffic of the Android apps you choose. No root, no server, and nothing else on the phone is touched.

Make any network misbehave.

Five controls, per-app rules and a live view of every connection — in one small app that stays out of the way of everything else.

Only the apps you pick

Everything else keeps its normal connection. Tick the apps under test and leave the rest of the phone alone.

Five real controls

Download, upload, latency, jitter and packet loss. Set them by slider or load a preset — and change them while the tunnel is running.

Watch the wire

A live rate chart, every open connection with its host and bytes, a packet capture and a log for when something won't connect.

Every connection, in plain sight.

Traffic from the selected apps is terminated locally, so the app can show you what would otherwise be invisible — and let you act on it.

  • App, host, port, bytes and whether a connection was blocked.
  • Search, filter by app, or show blocked attempts only.
  • Turn any connection into a rule: block the app or the host.
  • Record to .pcap for Wireshark, or export the list as CSV.

Five numbers,
one connection.

Speeds are logarithmic and the last notch means unlimited. All selected apps share the session values unless one is given its own profile.

Download 10 kbps – 50 Mbps

Caps data delivered to the selected apps.

Upload 10 kbps – 50 Mbps

Caps data sent by the selected apps.

Latency 0 – 2,000 ms

Added to every round trip, half each way.

Jitter 0 – 500 ms

Random variation of latency, per packet.

Packet loss 0 – 30%

Share of packets dropped at random.

Automate

Put the throttle in your test script.

Drive the tunnel from adb, a Quick Settings tile or a launcher shortcut. No screen taps in the middle of a run.

  • Quick SettingsStart and stop from the shade, using the saved settings.
  • ShortcutsLong-press the app icon for Start and Stop throttling.
  • ScenariosTrain through a tunnel, Elevator ride, Flaky Wi-Fi — or build your own sequence.
adb · start a session 
$ adb shell am start \
  -n com.rangandale.appthrottler/.control.ControlActivity \
  -a com.rangandale.appthrottler.action.START \
  --es profile 3G
STARTSTOPSCENARIOCANCEL_SCENARIORESET_CONNECTIONS

VPN permission must be granted once in the app. Names are matched case-insensitively; a scenario such as “Elevator ride” needs quoting.

No root. No server.
Nothing leaves the phone.

The throttle is a local VPN that only touches the apps you select. There is no account, no analytics, and no traffic routed through us. The source is yours to read.

No root

Runs as a local VPN on stock Android 5.0 and newer.

Local only

Traffic is relayed straight to the internet. Nothing is sent to a third party.
